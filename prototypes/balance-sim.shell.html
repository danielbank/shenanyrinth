Shenanyrinth balance simulation

The question (Balance simulation): which stats, items and builds are overpowered or useless, and does a 2–6 Player game really fit 20–40 minutes? Bots play complete games under every rule decided on the map: Pivots and Lost, Torchlight, Clashes with Press and Retreat, Shakedowns and Rams, Meddle, Sway and Resist, the four Town buildings, Loot, Deeds, the Doom Track and the Guardian. Every number is editable below, so you can try a tuning and see what it does.

How to read it: bots are decent but not perfect. They plan each move over the Tiles they can see, fight Mobs when the odds are worth it, bank Gold when their Pack gets heavy, and jab at whoever is leading. Each bot plays one build: its 6 setup points plus a shopping list. Game length in minutes comes from a timing guess (15 s a Turn, 12 s per Exchange, and so on, all editable). Treat the minutes as relative, not gospel.
