Fairness per Keep

Town movement is free: up to 1d6 (+ optional DEX) squares in any non-diagonal direction, and you use any building just by passing through it. Gate and Store are distances to the nearest one, in squares. The average roll with no DEX is 3.5. Loop is the errand loop: come in through the best Gate → your Keep (Deposit, heal) → all 4 stores → leave by the best Gate, taking the shortest order.
