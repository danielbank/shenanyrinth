Does Tile-path movement with a Pivot Limit feel fun and tense?

Play the Movement rules on a Forest-like or Dungeon-like board. Every square can be entered; a Tile's Exits decide how you leave it. Leaving in a direction other than your Heading is a Pivot. You get a few Pivots per Turn, and needing one you don't have makes you Lost. The dice roll must be moved in full, while DEX squares are optional. The walkthroughs show the tricky cases, and the simulator measures how often you get Lost.

Verdict (Movement feel prototype): the mechanic works. A base Pivot Limit of 1 was too harsh (the outer loop of the ring burned every Pivot on its corners), so the base is now 2. The "Pivot before moving" item may not U-turn. Real boards are randomly generated each game. The walkthroughs still use a limit of 1 to show the edge cases.
